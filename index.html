Research

I study how medical imaging models can make better use of scarce annotations and remain reliable across domains. My work connects three themes:

  • Few-shot and cross-domain segmentation. Prototype learning, regional representations, semantic matching, domain robustness, and uncertainty-aware prompting for medical images.
  • Medical image reconstruction and enhancement. Deep image priors, unsupervised MR denoising, and reconstruction and analysis of MR/CT data.
  • Foundation models and clinician interaction. Adaptation to medical imaging, uncertainty estimation, and learning from expert feedback on rare or unfamiliar cases.
